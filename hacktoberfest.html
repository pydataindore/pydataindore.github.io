Questions

Frequently Asked

Do I need to know how to code?
Some coding experience helps since this is a build event, but you don't need to be an expert. Teams benefit from diverse skills — design, research, project management, and domain expertise are all valuable.
Do I need a team?
No. You can participate solo or form a team of up to 4. We'll have a team-formation session at the start if you show up without one. Note that you'll enter your team details on the registration form, so sort out your team before registering if you can.
Is this only for students?
No. MLH Hack Days are open to everyone — students, professionals, freelancers, and career-switchers. All skill levels are welcome.
How do I register?
Every team member must register individually on the official MLH event page — registering one person for the whole team is not enough. It's required for check-in, swag, and eligibility, and a Meetup RSVP alone is not sufficient.
Can I use AI tools like ChatGPT, Copilot, or Gemini?
Yes, but you must disclose all AI tool usage in your submission. Your README should clearly document what was AI-generated vs. what you built. Undisclosed AI usage means disqualification.
What should I bring?
Your laptop, charger, and any hardware you want to hack with. Charging points are limited, so come fully charged and ideally carry a power bank. Keep a mobile hotspot handy as a backup connection.
How is this different from a regular meetup?
This is a build event, not a talk event. You show up, form a team, and build something in a single focused day. Demos and judging happen at the end. Think science fair, not conference.
