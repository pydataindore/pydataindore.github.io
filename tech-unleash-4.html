About the Event

What is Tech Unleash 4.0?

Tech Unleash 4.0 is the flagship orientation and tech fest of Google Developer Group on Campus, IET DAVV — the day the new "server" opens for the year. It brings students together for a mix of industry talks, live performances and arcade games, and sets the tone for a year of building. The 4.0 edition ran on a Minecraft-inspired theme: a new world loading, waiting to be built.

Across the day, speakers from Deloitte, PwC, Engineer Sahab and Shine On Stage covered entrepreneurship, building with AI, how modern apps really work from mobile to cloud, and the craft of communication — bookended by team performances, networking and games.

Why We Partnered

PyData Indore exists to grow the data and Python community in Central India. Tech Unleash 4.0 put a room full of Indore's student builders in one place, right at the start of their year — so we were glad to help more of them hear about it. Being a Community Partner meant we amplified the event; it didn't mean we ran it.

The Host

Google Developer Group on Campus, IET DAVV

GDGoC IET DAVV is a student developer community at the Institute of Engineering & Technology, DAVV, Indore. It runs workshops, hackathons and talks that connect students with developer tools and the wider tech ecosystem. Tech Unleash is its flagship orientation, and the 4.0 edition welcomed a new cohort into that community.

PyData Indore has partnered with GDGoC IET DAVV before — most recently as a Community Partner and mentor for their Hack'Vento 2K26 hackathon. Tech Unleash 4.0 continued that collaboration.

All event details on this page come from the organisers' official announcements. For results, photos or anything else about the event, check the GDGoC IET DAVV channels.
