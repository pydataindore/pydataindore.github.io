How It Works

  • Contact us with your interest and what you'd like to sponsor
  • We identify the vendors or services your support will cover
  • You pay the vendor directly — we don't handle any money
  • We confirm the support and credit you publicly
  • Share your logo and any materials for visibility
  • We keep you in the loop on community updates!

We keep things transparent and simple — no financial transactions go through PyData Indore.

Ready to Partner with Us?

We don't accept money directly — sponsors pay vendors on our behalf. Reach out and we'll figure out together what you can support and how to make it count.
