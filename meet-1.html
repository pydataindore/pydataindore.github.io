About the Event

Our First Official Meetup

Explore how AI goes beyond prompts to think, plan, and execute real-world tasks, creating value for your organisation. This is a practical, beginner-friendly session on building agentic AI with Python — no prior AI experience required.

Join us at InfoBeans Foundation for an engaging morning of learning, networking, an interactive quiz, and surprise prizes. Open to everyone — data professionals, developers, students, and curious minds alike.

▸ Practical introduction to Agentic AI
▸ Learn how AI moves from prompts to real workflows
▸ Networking with data professionals, developers & students
▸ Beginner-friendly, hands-on concepts
▸ Bonus: Interactive Quiz + Surprise Prizes 🎁
