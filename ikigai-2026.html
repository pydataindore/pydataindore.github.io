About this partnership

PyData Indore was a Community Partner for IKIGAI 2026 — not an organiser or co-host. The event was organised by the Department of CSIT & CSE(CY) at AITR, Indore, in collaboration with IIT Ropar.

Registration, screening, logistics, judging, prizes, and any fees were handled entirely by the IKIGAI 2026 team. Please direct all event questions — including results and certificates — to them via the official event site.

PyData Indore's own events remain free to attend, as always.

About the Event

What is IKIGAI 2026?

A national-level innovation hackathon that brought together India's brightest minds to build AI-powered solutions for real-world challenges. Over 36 hours, teams took an idea from problem statement to working prototype, with mentorship from academics and industry along the way.

It ran in two stages: a nationwide online registration and screening round, followed by an offline 36-hour finale on the AITR campus in Indore for the shortlisted teams, 21–23 August 2026.

▸ 36-hour National Level Hackathon
▸ Organised by Dept. of CSIT & CSE(CY), AITR Indore
▸ In collaboration with IIT Ropar
▸ 40–50 teams · 160–250 participants, nationwide
▸ ₹1,03,000 prize pool
Why We Partnered

PyData Indore exists to grow the data and Python community in Central India. IKIGAI 2026 put a national-scale AI build stage right here in Indore — so we were glad to help more local builders hear about it. Being a Community Partner meant we amplified the event; it didn't mean we ran it.

Format

A Two-Phase Journey

Designed to test ideas first, then execution and real-world impact. Both phases have now concluded.

Phase 01

Registration & Screening

Online · Nationwide

Was open to students across engineering and technology disciplines from institutions nationwide.

  • Teams registered online
  • Submitted a problem statement preference
  • Presented a proposed solution approach
  • Evaluated on innovation, feasibility and impact

Round 1 registration closed 26 July 2026

Phase 02

National Hackathon Finale

Offline · AITR Campus, Indore

Shortlisted teams competed in an intensive 36-hour innovation sprint to build and present functional solutions, 21–23 August 2026.

  • Mentorship & technical guidance
  • Infrastructure & development support
  • Networking opportunities
  • Evaluation by an expert and industry panel

Who Can Take Part

Eligibility & Teams

IKIGAI 2026 was open to UG and PG students from institutions across India. Teams were 2–4 members, and the organisers encouraged interdisciplinary teams — one team submitted one solution.

▸ Team size: 2–4 members
▸ Open to UG & PG students
▸ Interdisciplinary teams encouraged
▸ One team → one solution
▸ Nationwide participation

Final deliverables

Teams that made it through to the finale presented four things to the jury:

▸ A working prototype
▸ A solution demonstration
▸ A jury presentation
▸ An impact assessment
