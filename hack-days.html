About the Event

Build. Ship. Repeat.

PyData Indore Hack Days is a focused, single-day sprint where you turn an idea into a working prototype before sundown. No overnight marathons — just one high-energy day of hacking, mentorship, and demos with the local data & Python community.

Whether you're writing your first Python script or shipping models to production, you'll find a team, a problem worth solving, and people to learn from. Come with an idea or find one here.

What are MLH Hack Days?

Major League Hacking (MLH) is the official student hackathon league, powering thousands of events worldwide. Hack Days are short, local, beginner-friendly hackathons run by community organizers — like PyData Indore — with MLH support.

  • Backed by MLH's global hackathon program
  • Welcoming to first-time hackers
  • Recognition & swag from MLH

Questions

Frequently Asked

Who can participate?
Anyone keen to build — students and working professionals alike. All skill levels are welcome, including complete beginners.
Do I need prior hackathon or AI experience?
Not at all. Hack Days are designed to be beginner-friendly. If you can write a bit of Python (or are willing to learn on the day), you'll be fine. Mentors will be around to help.
How much does it cost?
It's completely free to participate. Just register on MLH and show up.
Can I participate solo or do I need a team?
Both work. You can hack solo or form a team of up to 4 members. Note that you'll need to enter your team details — team name and members — when you fill out the registration form, so sort out your team before registering.
What should I bring?
Your laptop, charger (and ideally a power bank), and a valid photo ID. Charging points are limited, so come fully charged. Wi-Fi will be provided, but keep a backup internet connection (mobile hotspot) handy just in case.
Is there a fixed theme?
The theme is open — build anything powered by Python or data. Check the Tracks & Ideas section for inspiration.
Can I use existing code or AI tools?
You should build your project during the event, but you may use open-source libraries, frameworks, and AI coding assistants. Just be ready to explain your work to the judges.
How do I register?
Register on the official MLH event page. If the MLH link isn't loading for you, you can register on our Meetup event page instead. Either way, you'll be asked a few quick questions, including whether you're participating individually or as a team.
